ANNUAL FACILITY FEE ELIGIBILITY FIX

Facility fees now apply from the student's joining calendar year regardless of
joining month. Example: a student joining in October 2026 owes the 2026 facility
fee even if its annual due date was January. No fee is charged for 2025.
Existing students continue to owe subsequent annual fees. Exam-term eligibility
is unchanged. Existing payments and accounting inclusion are preserved; a class
transfer does not create another annual facility fee for the same year.

CPANEL FILE MANAGER - NO TERMINAL REQUIRED

1. Upload facility-fee-annual-eligibility-fix.zip into the Laravel application root:
   /home/bicpolga/public_html/system.bicpolgahawela.lk/

2. Extract it there and overwrite matching files. The ZIP contains app/ and
   resources/ folders. Do not extract inside public/ or an extra wrapper folder.

3. In that same application folder, open storage/framework/views/ and delete
   only the compiled .php files inside. Keep the folder and .gitignore.

4. Hard-refresh Exam & Facility Fees (Command + Shift + R on Mac, Ctrl + F5 on
   Windows). Choose Facility Fee and the required installment year. Students
   joining in any month of that year now show the annual balance and can be
   included in individual or bulk payments. Existing class, search and student
   status filters still apply. Use Include inactive / alumni when required.
