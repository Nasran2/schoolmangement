EXAM FEE TERM DETAILS FIX

Exam payment details identify the paid term, for example:
  Exam Fee - Term 1 (2026)
  Exam Fee - Term 2 (2026)

This applies to the daily ledger, revenue reports, PDF/export notes, Revenue
manage list, financial details and cash/bank/cheque history. Printed exam bills
show Term paid instead of Installment due.

When multiple terms are paid through bulk collection, every term retains its
own payment and bill. Each report entry identifies the term it settles, with
its correct amount and bill reference. Transactions are not combined into a
single bill by this display update.

Existing linked receipts use their saved calendars without rewriting stored
notes, amounts, dates or bill numbers. Staff notes are retained. Converted
quarterly receipts use the mapped terms. Earlier quarterly schedules use the
chronological charge number within the due year. Configured terms spanning two
calendar years keep their saved term number. Unassigned receipts retain their
original notes rather than guessing a term from their payment date.
