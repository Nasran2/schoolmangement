WHERE TO COLLECT

Revenue -> Collect Exam Fee
Revenue -> Collect Facility Fee

Search a name or admission number across all classes, or choose a class before
searching. Click Search students, then select the student from the results on
the left. A unique matching student is selected automatically. The right panel
shows only the selected fee type's dues, recorded payments and pending cheques.
The search results use the student's current class. Inactive/alumni require the
explicit checkbox.

Earlier years are included by default. Uncheck Include earlier years to see only
the chosen installment year. Select Collect on a term or annual fee to choose a
different installment, then enter the amount, payment date and method. The
oldest collectable installment is selected automatically. Partial amounts are
supported. Pending cheques reserve their amounts; paid fees cannot be charged
again. Saving returns to the selected student with refreshed balances.

Include in revenue & ledger is checked by default. Uncheck only when recording
a historical payment whose income should not be counted again. Existing payment
edit/delete tools remain available in the fee register.

FORMER CLASS STUDENTS

The Exam & Facility Fees register and its PDF now show only current class
members by default. Include former class students allows their historical class
records to be viewed. Old payments and balances are retained. The collection
page for an individual student still includes that student's applicable earlier
class dues so a cashier can settle them without adding former students to the
current class roster. Dashboard financial calculations are preserved.

PREVIOUS FIXES RETAINED

- A student joining anytime through a configured term's ending date owes that
  term and following terms; completed terms before admission do not apply.
- Annual facility fees apply from the joining calendar year regardless of month.
- Individual, bulk and historical payments share the same balance calculations.
- The standalone term/bulk controls and guarded script loader are included.
