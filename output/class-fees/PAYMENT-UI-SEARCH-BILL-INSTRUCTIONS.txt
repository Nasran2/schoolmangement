HOW TO USE

Revenue -> Collect Exam Fee / Collect Facility Fee

- Type a name, admission number or phone number. Suggestions appear immediately
  after a short typing pause. Choose All classes or a specific current class.
  Arrow keys + Enter select a result; Escape dismisses suggestions.
- Select a student to show their fee-specific dues in the Billing Summary on
  the right. Term and annual eligibility use the existing shared billing rules.
- Change Payment Category to switch between exam, facility and ordinary revenue
  categories without losing the selected student. The monthly form also now
  includes the exam/facility categories and opens their installment collector.
- Choose an installment, enter a full or partial amount and payment date, then
  choose Cash, Bank Transfer or Cheque using the payment method cards.
- Save & view bill opens the printable bill. Save payment returns to collection.
  Bill numbering follows the existing automatic/manual setting. Manual bill
  numbers must be unique. Bill previews are reserved only on actual saving.
- Revenue & ledger inclusion is checked by default. Uncheck for historical
  completed payments that should settle dues without adding income again.
  Historical entries do not generate a new revenue bill.

CHEQUE RULES

Cheque date, number and bank are required. Name on cheque is optional.
A cheque starts on hold, reserves the installment balance, and stays outside
collected income until passed. Returning a pending cheque reopens collection.
A clearance date cannot be before the cheque date or later than today.
The daily ledger uses the clearance date. Cheques remain bank income, not cash.
Pending cheques cannot be entered as settled historical payments or have their
revenue tracking removed. Already cleared cheques can still be corrected through
the existing fee payment editor.

BILLS

Print Receipt is available on the generated bill. Exam bills show their term
and installment date; facility bills show the facility year. Both use the correct
fee label rather than Monthly fee. Pending-cheque bills include a warning inside
the printable receipt. Cashiers with only revenue.add may print their own bills;
revenue.manage retains access to all bills.
