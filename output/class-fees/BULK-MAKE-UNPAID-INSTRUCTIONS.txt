BULK MAKE PAID FEES UNPAID

WHERE TO FIND IT

Exam & Facility Fees > Bulk payments > Make unpaid
Or choose Bulk make unpaid directly from the fee register header.

HOW TO USE

1. Choose the fee year and load it.
2. Select a class, or search by student name/admission number across classes.
3. Choose Term 1, Term 2, Term 3, all exam terms, Facility fee, or all fees.
4. Select individual students or Select all available students, then click
   Add selected to reversal list. You can add students from several classes.
5. Review the exact receipts, historical entries, amounts and accounting effect.
6. Enter the reason and click Make [number] fees unpaid.

WHAT HAPPENS

All active payments for each selected student fee are cancelled. Its paid balance
is reopened. Revenue receipts are removed from income and the ledger; historical
payments change only the student fee balance. Bill numbers and audit history stay
available. The reason is stored in the batch and individual cancellation audits.
This corrects mistaken payments; it does not record a physical cash refund.

Paid and partially paid fees are supported, including several receipts settling
one fee. Pending cheques on the selected partly paid fee are also cancelled so
those funds no longer reserve the balance. Other terms and unselected students
are unaffected. Prepaid future fees reopen but remain Upcoming until their start.

Receipts with refunds or waivers, or unresolved unsupported cheque states, show a
review explanation and need individual handling in Revenue first. They cannot be
added by select all. The whole batch is checked again before saving. If a reviewed
payment changed, nothing in that batch is reversed; reload and review it again.
Repeating the same successful submission cannot reverse later replacement payments.
The existing revenue.delete permission is required. Up to 500 student fees fit in
one batch. Include inactive/alumni to find their earlier paid fees. Original-class
payments remain available under the class owning their receipts.

CPANEL FILE MANAGER - NO TERMINAL NEEDED

This patch is for the existing term/bulk-payment installation shown in your screen.
No database migration, SQL import, Composer install or asset build is needed.

1. Upload bulk-make-unpaid-update.zip to the Laravel application root:
   /home/bicpolga/public_html/system.bicpolgahawela.lk/

2. Extract there and overwrite matching files. Apply all files together, including
   the new ClassFeeReversalService, bulk-unpaid view and public/js/class-fees.js.
   Do not extract inside public/ or an extra wrapper folder.

3. In storage/framework/views/, delete only the compiled .php files.
   Preserve the folder and .gitignore.

4. If bootstrap/cache/routes-v7.php exists, delete that route cache file so the
   new bulk reversal endpoint and updated permission checks are loaded.
   Preserve the folder and other cache files.

5. Hard-refresh Exam & Facility Fees > Bulk payments. The Make unpaid option
   appears for administrators and users with revenue.delete.
