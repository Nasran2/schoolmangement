TERM FEE ADMISSION ELIGIBILITY FIX

Previously, students joining after the term starting date were excluded.
For three-term calendars, students now owe the term in which they join and all
subsequent terms. Admission on the term ending date is included. Terms which
ended before the admission date do not apply.

Example: Term 3 runs September 1 to December 31, 2026. A student joining in
October 2026 owes Term 3, while Terms 1 and 2 remain not applicable.
The saved term ending date is used, including custom dates and terms spanning
two calendar years. Both individual and bulk collection use the same rule.
Existing students who transferred classes retain the class responsible for the
term at its starting date. A former class may therefore correctly show Not
applicable when that term belongs to another class.

This ZIP also retains the annual facility rule: joining in any month of a year
makes that year's facility fee applicable, without adding earlier years.
Existing payments, receipts and revenue/ledger settings are preserved.
Legacy exam schedules without an explicit term calendar retain their due-date
eligibility. Enable three editable terms and save dates to use term eligibility.

   /home/bicpolga/public_html/system.bicpolgahawela.lk/

   application root, not inside public/ or an extra wrapper folder.

3. Open storage/framework/views/ in that application folder. Delete only the
   compiled .php files inside. Keep the views folder and .gitignore.

4. Hard-refresh the Exam & Facility Fees page (Command + Shift + R on Mac,
   Ctrl + F5 on Windows). Existing class, student and status filters still apply.

No terminal commands, migration, database reset or asset build is required.
Do not re-enter any payments which are already recorded.
