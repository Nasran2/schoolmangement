WHAT IS INCLUDED
- Instant student name/admission search, across all classes or one selected class.
- Year/all-years, paid/unpaid/partial/pending cheque, inactive/alumni, former-class,
  exam-term selection and facility-inclusion filters.
- All students includes students without selected fees. Former-class students
  appear only if their filter is checked; inactive/alumni also require their filter.
- Individual Pay popup with amount/date, cash/bank/cheque, revenue & ledger option,
  original billing rules and a View / print bill link after a revenue payment.
- Individual Make unpaid popup requiring a reason, plus existing Edit payment links.
- Bulk make unpaid, including class/search/select-all, term selection and reason.
- PDF and CSV downloads include every matching student, across all pages.

Reversals retain cancelled receipts, bill references and audit history. Revenue
payments are removed from income and the fee becomes due again. Historical
payments affect only student fee history. This does not record physical cash
refunds. Receipts with refunds/waivers require individual review. Pending cheques
remain unpaid until passed. Reopening a partially paid fee also cancels any
pending cheque on that same fee after review in the popup.

This package contains the full local changes for this request and the immediately
preceding bulk make-unpaid request. Deploy every included file together.
Existing data is preserved. No live student payments were changed during testing.
